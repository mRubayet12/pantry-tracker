# Pantry Tracker

CEN 4020 Software Engineering I, Fall 2026
Mohammad Rubayet Islam

## The problem

Households throw out groceries because people forget what they bought or miss the expiration date. Pantry Tracker is an app that keeps an inventory of the food in a home and alerts people before items expire. A stretch goal is suggesting recipes from what is currently in stock.

## Assignment 2: Requirements Gathering

The requirements backlog is in [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md). It represents the outcome of an initial pass of requirements elicitation and contains:

- 10 functional and 4 non-functional requirements
- metadata tags on every requirement (MoSCoW priority, release, estimate)
- dependencies between requirements, as a column in the summary table and as a dependency graph
- a user story and acceptance criteria for each functional requirement
- what was left out of scope, assumptions, and open questions for the next elicitation pass
